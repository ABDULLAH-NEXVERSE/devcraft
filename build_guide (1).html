1. Executive Summary

This report gives the developer the exact structure, content direction, UX instructions, SEO requirements and technical checklist needed to build the DevCraft website from scratch.

DevCraft is a software development company offering web development, mobile app development, custom software, e-commerce development, UI/UX design, AI/ML solutions and ongoing maintenance & support. Unlike Duralean (a procurement company that needed to move away from a narrow product identity), DevCraft needs the opposite: a website that makes capability, proof and trust visible within the first screen, so a visitor immediately understands what DevCraft builds, for whom, and how to start a project.

Guiding flow for the whole site: Capability → Proof → Trust → Action — what DevCraft does, what it has already built (ProRota, NexEats, client work), why DevCraft (two-team 24/7 model, real tech stack, sector experience), and a clear way to start a conversation (quote request or free consultation).
Brand positioningA full-stack software development partner — not a freelancer or single-service shop — that designs, builds and maintains web, mobile and AI-powered products for regulated and operationally complex industries.
NavigationLead with Services and Work/Products before a long feature list; keep Technologies and Industries supporting, not competing, with the main story.
HomepageOpen on capability + proof (own products, client logos), not a generic "we build websites" hero.
Proof pagesGive ProRota and NexEats their own Products page — real, live, operated software is DevCraft's strongest credibility asset.
Contact/lead-genAdd both a structured quote-request form and a free-consultation booking option, routed by project type.
SEOTarget web/mobile/custom software development keywords blended with the specific industries DevCraft serves (logistics, procurement, compliance, fintech, healthcare, e-commerce).
ClaimsOnly publish team size, years in operation, client names and results that DevCraft has confirmed; mark anything else "to be confirmed."

2. Correct DevCraft Positioning

The website must communicate that DevCraft is a two-team, full-stack development partner that ships and maintains real software — not a portfolio-only freelance page. Own products (ProRota, NexEats) are the strongest proof point and should be treated as flagship case studies, not a footnote.

Use this positioning

  • Full-stack software development partner
  • Two-team, follow-the-sun delivery model (UK + Pakistan)
  • Builders of real, live products (ProRota, NexEats, NexRider)
  • Specialists across web, mobile, custom software, AI/ML and e-commerce
  • Long-term maintenance and support partner, not a one-off shop

Do not use this positioning

  • Generic "we design websites" freelancer studio
  • Single-service agency (web-only or design-only)
  • Portfolio site with no proof of ongoing support
  • Overseas-only outsourcing vendor with no UK presence
  • Unverifiable "best," "leading," or "#1" claims
